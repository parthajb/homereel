# Code of conduct

HomeReel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.

Don't post other people's personal details. That includes what tends to slip into screenshots and logs: IP
addresses, Wi-Fi names, account emails, device serial numbers and people's names. Blur them first.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential.
