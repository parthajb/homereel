Settings

With a free TMDB key, the phone fills in each movie's poster, backdrop, description, genres, cast, director and age rating by itself. Add an OMDb key too for the real IMDb rating; without one, TMDB's own score is shown. The TV never goes online: the phone fetches everything once and keeps it.

Privacy: movie titles and years are sent to TMDB, and IMDb ids to OMDb, to find their details. Nothing else leaves the phone. The keys stay on the phone; this page can only see whether they're set.

Family

Each profile has its own Continue watching, My List and “Because you watched” rows. A kids profile only shows titles rated for children (U, U/A 7+, G, PG…) or ones you mark for kids, never Private.

Private folder

Videos in Private are hidden from every TV and the web app, and from the rest of this page: they only show after Open Private videos below. Unlock them for a while to watch on the TV; they lock again by themselves. Kids profiles never see them.

Torrent downloads

Only download what you're allowed to, such as public-domain and Creative Commons films, or copies of your own. While it downloads, the phone exchanges pieces with the other people sharing that torrent, and they can see your internet address. HomeReel never opens ports on your router, and stops sharing once a download is in the library.

Backup

One file with everything you've typed in or looked up, everyone's progress, profiles and My Lists. Not the videos themselves, the keys or the PIN. Restore it on a new phone, or after resetting this one; files are matched by name and size.

App updates

Update the apps without a cable or ADB: upload a newer .apk built from this project. TVs offer it the next time HomeReel opens on them, and the phone shows Install update. Only HomeReel apps signed with the same key as this one are accepted.
